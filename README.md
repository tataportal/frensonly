# Frens Only

**A Computational Culture Centre for synthetic media, AI cinema, 3D visual culture, and digital presence.**

[![Frens Only](assets/frens-home-poster.jpg)](https://frensonly.club)

[Visit Frens Only](https://frensonly.club)

## The idea

Frens Only is a cultural platform for looking at the images, spaces, identities, and stories emerging from computation.

It operates across publishing, moving image, objects, digital experiences, and research. The project treats AI and 3D not as visual effects, but as cultural media with their own politics, aesthetics, and ways of seeing.

## Creative direction

The identity borrows the scale and confidence of a cultural institution while remaining native to the internet. A black-and-white field, monumental typography, cinematic image treatment, precise bilingual composition, and restrained navigation give the work room to feel unfamiliar.

The visual system is deliberately content-led:

- moving image establishes atmosphere
- typography behaves as architecture
- long-form text retains editorial clarity
- objects and editions extend the institution into physical culture

## Platform

- editorial and cultural programming
- synthetic media and AI cinema
- 3D art and digital presence
- bilingual and multilingual publishing
- objects and limited editions
- responsive, video-led web experience
