Position / 立场

The image comes first.

We are not here to explain the machine. We are here to shape what it makes visible.

我们不是为了说明机器而存在。我们关心的是它让什么变得可见，以及这张图像如何获得气候、节奏与记忆。

About / 关于

Images move through cinema, software, 3D space, and the internet.

Frens Only is a Computational Culture Centre for synthetic media, AI cinema, and 3D visual culture. Between Taipei, Lima, Los Angeles, and online, it shapes images with atmosphere, rhythm, and presence.

Frens Only 是一个计算文化中心，专注于合成媒体、AI 影像与 3D 视觉文化。在台北、利马、洛杉矶与线上空间之间，它塑造带有气氛、节奏与存在感的影像。

Creativity Over Tools 以意为先，技术为用

The image begins with intention: a tension, a mood, a world trying to appear.

影像始于意图：一种张力、一种气氛、一个正在显现的世界。

Sharpen The Instrument 工欲善其事，必先利其器

Speed and precision matter when they create more room for visual judgment.

速度与精度的意义，在于为视觉判断留下更多空间。

Beyond The Form 得意忘形

The final piece should feel inevitable: an image with its own weather, logic, and pulse.

最终作品应像某种必然：一张拥有自身气候、逻辑与脉搏的图像。

Output / 产出

Output

We make images that move between cinema, software, 3D space, and the internet.

我们制作介于电影、软件、三维空间与互联网之间的影像。有些成为影片，有些成为世界，有些只是一张准确的图像，却能改变整个项目的气氛。

01 / Moving Image

Short films, loops, edits, teasers, music pieces, and audiovisual fragments built with direction before technique.

02 / Synthetic Worlds

Objects, environments, characters, surfaces, and atmospheres shaped through AI, 3D, CGI, and post-production.

03 / Visual Systems

A coherent language of stills, motion, references, formats, and rules so a project can keep expanding without losing its center.

04 / Digital Presence

Images for screens, releases, social space, performances, objects, and moments where culture is experienced online.
