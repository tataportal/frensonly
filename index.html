About / 关于

Frens Only is an image studio working across synthetic media, AI cinema, 3D visual culture, and internet-born aesthetics.

We create visual material for films, brands, releases, objects, online worlds, and cultural projects.

Creativity Over Tools 以意为先，技术为用

The image begins with intention: a tension, a mood, a world on the verge of appearing.

Sharpen the Instrument 工欲善其事，必先利其器

Speed and precision matter because they leave more room for visual judgment.

Beyond the Words 意在言外

The final piece should feel inevitable: an image with its own weather, logic, and pulse.

Output / 产出

Our output moves across motion, synthetic worlds, key visuals, visual systems, and digital presence.

我们的产出横跨动态影像、合成世界、关键视觉、视觉系统与数字呈现。

Moving Image

Short films, loops, teasers, campaign edits, logo reveals, audiovisual fragments, and social-first motion pieces.

Synthetic Worlds

Characters, environments, objects, machines, surfaces, atmospheres, and visual universes built through AI, 3D, CGI, and post-production.

Key Visuals

Hero images, campaign frames, covers, posters, launch visuals, product visuals, and art-direction pieces.

Visual Systems

Moodboards, shot language, style frames, reference systems, image rules, and continuity systems for projects that need to expand.

Digital Presence

Images and motion for websites, drops, exhibitions, performances, online objects, and internet-native projects.
